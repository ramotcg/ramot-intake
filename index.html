Our Story

R.A.M.O.T. was built on a simple conviction: the best financial partner isn't just experienced — they're someone you can trust.

R.A.M.O.T. was founded after years spent across public accounting, transaction advisory, private equity, and in-house financial leadership. Each role taught us something different about what makes a business tick — and what business owners actually need from their financial partners. The answer, more often than not, is simple: someone with the experience to guide them and who truly cares about the outcome.

We serve small businesses across industries including wholesale distribution, retail, professional services, real estate, and technology — both in the Chicago/Skokie area and nationwide through remote engagements.
